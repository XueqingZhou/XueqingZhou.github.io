## Short bio

I work on multimodal synthetic data and algorithm evaluation at Ant Group. My interests center on long-context modeling and ML systems (algorithm → kernel → serving), with recent work on PDE-inspired architectures and mechanistic diagnostics for multimodal Transformers.
