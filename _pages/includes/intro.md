## About Me

I work on multimodal synthetic data and algorithm evaluation at Ant Group. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Fudan University, advised by Prof. [Yu-Gang Jiang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=f3_FP8AAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) (IEEE Fellow) and Prof. [Jingjing Chen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DfWdqzQAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN&oi=ao), where I was a member of the [Fudan Vision and Learning Laboratory (FVL)](https://fvl.fudan.edu.cn/). I received my B.Eng. in Civil Engineering from Tongji University.

I am interested in **long-context modeling**, **ML systems** (kernel fusion, serving stacks), **multimodal reasoning diagnostics**, and **system-aware evaluation**. If you are interested in collaborations, feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:alancurry1456@gmail.com).
