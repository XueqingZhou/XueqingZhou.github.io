## About Me

**Tagline.** Algorithm–system co-design for **long-context** and **efficient architectures**.

I am an Algorithm Engineer / Research Scientist at Ant Group, focusing on foundation-model research and deployment. My work lives at the intersection of **algorithm innovation** and **system efficiency**, with an emphasis on making long-context models both **scalable** and **servable**.

I am particularly interested in breaking the \(O(N^2)\) memory/compute wall in Transformers via theoretically grounded mechanisms and hardware-aware implementation. Recent work explores PDE-inspired token-mixing dynamics and system-oriented evidence (profiling/benchmarks), with practical tooling in stacks like **Triton** and **vLLM**.

**Research Interests**
- **Long-context & reasoning**: inference-time scaling, KV-cache behavior, long-prefill optimization
- **Efficient architectures**: PDE-driven dynamics, linear/structured attention, state-space style operators
- **Algorithm–system co-design**: fused kernels, profiling, serving-oriented evaluation
