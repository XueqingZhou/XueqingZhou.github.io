## About Me

**Tagline.** Algorithm–system co-design for **long-context** and **efficient architectures**.

I am an Algorithm Engineer / Research Scientist at Ant Group working on foundation models. My work lies at the intersection of **algorithm design** and **ML systems**, with a focus on making long-context models both **scalable** and **servable** in real-world deployments.

I am particularly interested in breaking the \(O(N^2)\) memory and compute wall of Transformers using theoretically grounded mechanisms and hardware-aware implementations. Recently I have been exploring PDE-inspired token-mixing dynamics and building evidence-first pipelines—profiling, benchmarks, and serving-style experiments—on top of stacks such as **Triton** and **vLLM**.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Fudan University, advised by Prof. [Yu-Gang Jiang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=f3_FP8AAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) (IEEE Fellow) and Prof. [Jingjing Chen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DfWdqzQAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN&oi=ao), where I was part of the [Fudan Vision and Learning Laboratory (FVL)](https://fvl.fudan.edu.cn/). Before that, I obtained my B.Eng. in Civil Engineering from Tongji University.

**Research Interests**
- **Long-context & reasoning**: inference-time scaling, KV-cache behavior, long-prefill optimization
- **Efficient architectures**: PDE-driven dynamics, linear/structured attention, state-space style operators
- **Algorithm–system co-design**: fused kernels, profiling, serving-oriented evaluation
