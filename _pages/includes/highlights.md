## Highlights

- **Multimodal reasoning diagnostics (2026)**: *Layer-wise PID + causal attention knockouts* to quantify “vision → language” transduction; **language-unique ≈ 82%** at the final prediction, **synergy < 2%**, and stable layer-wise fingerprints (**corr > 0.96**) across model variants. [[arXiv:2602.15580](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15580)]
- **Long-context architecture → systems evidence (EMNLP 2025)**: *Continuous-Time Attention* (PDE-guided token mixing) maintained as an **algorithm-to-systems portfolio** with Triton kernels, profiling traces, and serving-oriented benchmarks. [[Project](https://xueqingzhou.github.io/Continuous-Time-Attention/)] [[Code](https://github.com/XueqingZhou/Continuous-Time-Attention)]
- **Where to insert locality (2026)**: diffusion–attention **non-commutativity** as a structural diagnostic; early diffusion improves average **LRA +4.1** points, while post-attention diffusion degrades **−2.5** points. [[arXiv:2510.03272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03272)]
- **PDE lens for Transformer stabilizers (2024/2025)**: a continuous-dynamics probe explaining why residual connections and layer normalization act as mathematical stabilizers in deep Transformers. [[arXiv:2408.09523](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.09523)]
